# All or Everything Alliance Framework
### Not everything has been fleshed out (yet), but there will for certain be the following features:
- A multi-layered system of alliances, with each layer tailored to a specific organization/figure type or organization/figure preference.
- An influence-hardened system & contract to accept donations from wealthy figures or organizations without compromising integrity, something along the lines of "By donating you support this movement in its existing goals and means, and agree to provide monetary aid to further said goals and means."
- A consolidated central IP holding company coupled with a reimbursement system that personally rewards individuals submitting impactful innovations even for other companies in the alliance. 
- A highly streamlined and efficient system of political influence, managing everything from talent scouting & candidate screening to payment sums & interest alignment measures, and designed to:
  - Unify the interests of the state and alliance, providing regulatory immunity as well as a substantial competitive advantage for the alliance, thus a natural incentive to join said alliance, thus greater and further-reaching influence of the alliance over industry, and thus the necessary leverage to regulate said industry, improving the conditions of the encompassing region socially and economically, as both an end in itself and as a catalyst for greater social and thus political support.
  - Enable the passing of AoE-aligned legislation, designed to likewise improve the conditions of the region for the same reasons mentioned.
  - Facilitate the merging of state and org+industry alliance to form a full-fledged AoESS sector from top to bottom.

### Real-time movement decision-making structure

A mixture of mentalities structure separating constrictive, expansive, procedural, and exploitative mindsets into their own strategic division, with a central strategy selection mechanism and peripheral gated low-profile continuous (selected strategy-aligned) operational tactical and social maneuvering executors.

The strategy selector is fully context-oriented. It does not make decisions, it checks how well strategy matches context. All four divisions review each others' proposals and provide adversarial criticism that is then attached to the proposals critiqued.

All four proposals are then sent to the central strategy selector, which is provided a panoramic view of the political situation on the ground (to the extent that is possible).

The central strategy selector discards all criticisms irrelevant to the context and if it cannot find a strategy that sufficiently satisfies the criteria (which is the context) then it sends them back with only the relevant critiques still attached.

The criticisms simply make flaws more visible so the central context selector can spend less time trying to spot fundamental flaws, and instead do the easier job of eliminating irrelevant or weak criticisms and weighing the cost of effort (relatively consistent) and decision latency (context-specific) with the cost of premature action (carries greater potential moral liability if gone wrong, depends on risk level and context complexity).

This is then repeated. If a number of cycles (weighed by ratio of effort + latency compared to risk*(complexity/expertise)) pass without selection, the proposals are shuffled between divisions. First to the division least critical of it. If the same number of cycles pass again, it switches to the division most critical of it. If this number of cycles passes again, all policies/strategies are put together and the central strategy selector orchestrates a synthesis amongst all four divisions.

Has a separate opposition branch that deploys oppositional strategy. Conditionally retractable and subject to the above mixture-of-mentalities strategic center. Has low operational authority to avoid narrowing the mission into an existential fight against the existing state and corperations.

### Separation of system and movement

The movement establishes the system, it does not run it.

This prevents multiple known failure modes, including rule by inertia, ideological domination, and adversarially-unchecked/unaccountable system design (the tribal illusion of 'our guys will make the right choices' that prevents consideration for robust accountability measures, common alongside ideological domination especially). The system (AoESS) decides further movement funding (by default yes, citizen veto and fund reduction powers built in) and the movement can operate more efficiently, in a more streamlined, repeated manner because members are not caught up in governance duties and instead stay light on their feet for uninterrupted operations.
